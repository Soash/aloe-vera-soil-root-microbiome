# Raw Sequencing Data & Quality Control Metrics

This directory contains the paired-end 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing data (V4–V5 hypervariable region, 2×300 bp, Illumina MiSeq) for the *Aloe vera* root endosphere and rhizosphere soil soft-rot microbiome investigation.

---

## 1. Sample Inventory & Design

| Sample Identifier | Health Status | Microhabitat Compartment | Description | Total Raw Pairs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **`Disease_Root`** | Disease | Root (Endosphere / Rhizoplane) | Macerated root tissue from soft-rot symptomatic *Aloe vera* | 133,088 |
| **`Disease_Soil`** | Disease | Rhizosphere Soil | Soil adhering to roots of diseased *Aloe vera* | 162,118 |
| **`Healthy_Root`** | Healthy | Root (Endosphere) | Asymptomatic root biopsy from healthy *Aloe vera* | 146,804 |
| **`Healthy_Soil`** | Healthy | Rhizosphere Soil | Soil adjacent to root systems of healthy *Aloe vera* | 199,442 |

---

## 2. Confidentiality & Data Protection Notice

> **Data Protection Note:** Raw high-throughput FASTQ sequencing reads (`*.fastq.gz`) are restricted and excluded from public version control via `.gitignore` to preserve data confidentiality during ongoing manuscript preparation and peer review. FastQC HTML evaluation reports and multi-metric denoising statistics are preserved in `results/` for analytical verification.
